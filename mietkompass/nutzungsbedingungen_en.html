Services

The app helps private landlords in Switzerland to manage their tenancies. Based on the data entered, it calculates rent adjustments, deadlines, ancillary costs and end-of-tenancy statements, creates drafts of letters, reports and QR-bills and displays cantonal information. The app does not create tenancy agreements or official forms.

The governing rules are the Code of Obligations (CO), the Ordinance on the Lease and Usufructuary Lease of Residential and Commercial Premises (VMWG), cantonal law, the tenancy agreement and case law. These change. The rules and references stored in the app reflect the date shown in the app and may be out of date.
