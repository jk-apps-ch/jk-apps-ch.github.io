Services

The app helps private landlords in Switzerland to manage their tenancies. It calculates rent adjustments, deadlines and ancillary costs, creates letters, statements and QR-bills and displays cantonal rules.

No legal advice

The app is a tool. It does not replace legal advice. The governing rules are the Code of Obligations (CO), the Ordinance on the Lease and Usufructuary Lease of Residential and Commercial Premises (VMWG), cantonal law and case law. Check important steps such as rent increases or notices of termination before sending them. Rent increases and notices of termination by the landlord are only valid if made using the canton's official form.
