Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the iPhone app “Mietkompass”. It is based on the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

Controller

Joel Kirst
Email: jk.apps@icloud.com

Principle: your data stays on your iPhone

The app has no server of its own. Everything you enter is stored by the app only on your device: properties, units, tenancies, details of tenants, payments, expenses, documents, handover reports with photos and signatures and your landlord details. We as the provider have no access to this data.

If your iPhone creates a backup in iCloud or on a computer, the app's data is included in it. Apple's terms apply to such backups.

Sign in with Apple or Google

You can use the app with or without an account. If you sign in with Apple or Google, the app receives an identifier, your name and your email address from the respective provider, provided you share them. The app stores this information only in your iPhone's keychain and does not transmit it to us. The privacy policies of Apple (apple.com/legal/privacy) and Google (policies.google.com/privacy) apply to the sign-in itself. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process.

Requests to public bodies

To provide its functions, the app retrieves public data. The body concerned can see your device's IP address.

  • Address search and building data: the address you enter is sent to geo.admin.ch (Federal Office of Topography swisstopo). The building data comes from the Federal Register of Buildings and Dwellings.
  • Reference interest rate: retrieval of the public page of the Federal Office for Housing (bwo.admin.ch).
  • Swiss consumer price index: retrieval from the data portal of the Swiss National Bank (data.snb.ch).

No information about your tenancies is transmitted in the process.

Subscription and payments

You purchase and manage the “Mietkompass Pro” subscription via the App Store. Payment is processed by Apple. We do not receive any payment data. The app only checks on your device whether a valid subscription exists.

Notifications, camera and photos

The app schedules reminders as local notifications on your iPhone. The app only uses the camera and photos when you yourself scan a document or add an image. The files remain in the app on your device.

No advertising, no analytics

The app contains no advertising, no tracking and no third-party analytics tools.

Data of your tenants

If you enter personal data of your tenants, you are yourself responsible for processing it in accordance with the Federal Act on Data Protection. Only enter what you need for the tenancy and delete data you no longer need.

Deletion

You can delete individual entries at any time. Under “Account › Delete account” you can remove the sign-in and, if you wish, all of the app's data. If you remove the app from your iPhone, all of its data is deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data. As we do not store any data about you, all information is on your device, where you can view, change and delete it yourself. If you have any questions, you can contact us at jk.apps@icloud.com. You can also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

Changes

We may amend this privacy policy. The version currently published in the app applies.
