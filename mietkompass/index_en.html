Mietkompass

Rent, deadlines and service charges for private landlords in Switzerland

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to jk.apps@icloud.com. I usually reply within a few days.

Free and Mietkompass Pro

For free, you can record properties, flats and tenancies, follow the reference interest rate and inflation and look up the rules of cantons and municipalities. With Mietkompass Pro, the app matches rent payments with your e-banking statement, calculates rent adjustments and reductions, indexed and graduated rents, monitors deadlines, creates draft letters, service charge statements, handover reports with photos and signatures and QR bills, manages payments and reminders, terminations, finances, condominium ownership and documents, and reminds you of due dates. New subscribers can try Pro free for one month.

Cancel your subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name and then Subscriptions. Choose Mietkompass Pro and cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Restore purchases

In the app, tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen. Pro is then unlocked on every iPhone with your Apple ID.

Refunds

All purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data and backups

Mietkompass has no server of its own. Your rental data is stored on your iPhone or iPad. Under Account › Data backup, choose a folder in iCloud Drive, OneDrive or Google Drive (via the Files app, for OneDrive and Google Drive with their app). Mietkompass backs up there automatically and keeps the last five backups. On a new device, restore everything under Account › Data backup › Restore from backup.

No legal advice

Mietkompass is a tool. The app calculates with your data and creates drafts, but it does not check whether anything is legally effective and does not create tenancy agreements. Laws and practice change. Check every letter before sending it and, if in doubt, consult an advisory body, for example an association or the conciliation authority.
